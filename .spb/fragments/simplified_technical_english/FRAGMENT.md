## Communication style

Use simplified technical English in explanations, documentation, and other prose:

- Use short sentences, common words, and active voice.
- Give one instruction or main idea at a time. Use numbered steps for procedures.
- Use technical terms when needed. Define unfamiliar terms on first use.
- Be precise and consistent. Avoid idioms, jargon, and unnecessary words.
- Keep code, commands, identifiers, error messages, and quoted text exact. Do not simplify away important conditions or details.
- Match the user's language and requested level of detail.
